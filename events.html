Signature Monthly Tradition

First Friday Downtown Art Walk

On the first Friday of every month from 5:30 PM to 9:00 PM, Historic Downtown Bastrop throws open gallery doors, courtyard stages, print presses, and sidewalk easels—free and family-friendly.

Explore Participating Venues →

Suggested 4-Stop Stroll

  1. 5:30 PM • 904 Main St: Grab a map at Bastrop Museum & Visitor Center.
  2. 6:00 PM • Old Iron Bridge: Golden-hour sunset photos above the Colorado River.
  3. 6:45 PM • Main & Pine Co-ops: Meet painters, potters & relief printmakers.
  4. 8:00 PM • Spring & Chestnut: Curtain time at the 1889 Opera House or river deck acoustic music.
