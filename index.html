Where the Lost Pines meet a living canvas.

Thirty miles downriver from Austin, Bastrop pairs 1889 Victorian brick stages and converted glassblowing silos with world-renowned bronze foundries, wood-fired river clay, and front-porch Texas songcraft.

01 / Cultural Ecosystem

Small river town. Monumental creative soul.

From 2,000°F molten bronze pours on Highway 71 to quiet color-theory mornings and historic Main Street murals, Bastrop makes room for every discipline.

Bronze, Clay & Timber

Bastrop casts monumental public bronzes shipped nationwide, fires alluvial Colorado River clay with loblolly pine ash, and carves salvaged timber.
